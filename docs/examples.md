# Examples

Viewport renders from **Release the krack** in KracKill$ Vision v1.0, captured while the startup mix plays, with the workspace grid off. These 50 were picked from 180 presses for colour, detail and coverage, with at most 10 of any one form; every press is a real, unedited result. Each caption lists the form, the grid style, the Krack’d FX from top to bottom of the stack, and the Krack’d Vision look when one was released.

Each press changes only the selected layer. See [Release the krack](../README.md#release-the-krack) for what it sets and its limits.
